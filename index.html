ABOUT ME

More Than Numbers, Creating Financial Clarity.

I am Nasrul Hayati, an accounting and finance professional with an academic foundation in the Bachelor of Accountancy program at Universitas Terbuka. My work centers on organizing financial records with precision, preparing reliable reports, and supporting transparent business administration.

I believe that well-structured accounting goes beyond compliance—it provides clarity that empowers sound operational and strategic decisions. By combining methodical bookkeeping with data-driven financial analysis, I aim to turn raw figures into clear, actionable narratives.

Academic Background

Core Values
